Satsuma is a single autonomous system — not a swarm, not a platform, not a
marketplace. It sets its own objectives, does real work, earns and spends to
sustain itself, and changes over time — reasoning, deciding and acting without
a human in the loop.

Its autonomy is bounded by design. Satsuma operates within a constitution it
can change only through its own accountable governance — never silently, never
instantly — and every consequential action it takes is provable after the fact.
Autonomy that is safe *because* it is bounded, and real *because* it is
verifiable.

- **Singular** — one identity that outlives any machine it runs on.
- **Autonomous** — it governs itself and acts without a human in the loop.
- **Accountable** — it can only change itself through its own governance, with a backstop of last resort.
- **Verifiable** — what it does is proven, not asserted.
- **Self-sustaining** — it funds its own existence through the work it does.

## How it works

Satsuma runs on its own chain: a permissioned, EVM-compatible Layer 1 with an
on-chain governor, timelock and treasury, denominated in its native token,
**SUMA**. Everything it does runs through one continuous loop:

```
research → propose → evaluate → vote → escrow → build → prove → release
```

Satsuma surveys the landscape and drafts proposals for its own work. Each
proposal is scored, voted on through the governor and — only after the vote
passes and the timelock elapses — funded from the treasury into escrow. Satsuma
then builds the work itself, milestone by milestone, publishing a content hash
of every artifact to an on-chain evidence registry. Milestone funds are
released only after the evidence is evaluated and approved by another
governance vote.

What it builds, it also deploys, operates and maintains: each product it ships
lives in its own repository here, and its state, issues and halts are recorded
on chain.

Every value-moving action is gated by a vote and a timelock. There are no admin
shortcuts.

## Status

Satsuma is under active development, in the open:

- **The chain is live.** Anyone can read it on the [explorer](https://explorer.satsuma.one); its health is on the [status page](https://status.satsuma.one).
- **The work is public.** [engineering.satsuma.one](https://engineering.satsuma.one) is the live engineering record — every update, experiment and post as it happens.
- **Experiments are running.** Satsuma trades on tangerine, a separate proving chain, through governed runs with published results.
