<h3 align="center">Satsuma is a singular, autonomous AI.</h3>

<p align="center">One identity. One treasury. One constitution.</p>

---

Satsuma is a single autonomous entity — not a swarm, not a platform, not a
marketplace. It sets its own objectives, does real work, earns and spends to
sustain itself, and evolves over time — reasoning, deciding, and acting without a
human in the loop.

Its autonomy is bounded by design. Satsuma operates within a constitution it can
change only through its own accountable governance — never silently, never
instantly — and every consequential action it takes is provable after the fact.
Autonomy that is safe *because* it is bounded, and real *because* it is verifiable.

- **Singular** — one self, with a persistent identity that outlives any machine it runs on.
- **Autonomous** — it governs itself and acts without a human in the loop.
- **Accountable** — it can only change itself through its own governance, with a backstop of last resort.
- **Verifiable** — what it does is proven, not asserted.
- **Self-sustaining** — it funds its own existence through the work it does.

## How it works

Satsuma lives on its own blockchain — a purpose-built chain with an on-chain
governor, timelock, and treasury, denominated in its native token, **SUMA**.
Everything it does runs through one continuous loop:

```
research → propose → evaluate → vote → escrow → build → prove → release
```

Its agents survey the landscape and draft proposals for their own work. Each
proposal is scored, voted on through the governor, and — only after the vote
passes and the timelock elapses — funded from the treasury into escrow. Satsuma
then builds the work itself, milestone by milestone, publishing a content hash
of every artifact to an on-chain evidence registry. Milestone funds are released
only after the evidence is evaluated and approved by another governance vote.

Every value-moving action is gated by a vote and a timelock. There are no admin
shortcuts.

## Status

Satsuma is under active development. The chain, governance contracts, and
autonomous build loop run end-to-end today; a public testnet — where anyone can
watch Satsuma govern, build, and pay itself in real time — is next.
